How to read

The wheel ranks responsibility

Each wedge is one country in the selected year.

Countries are sorted by transition pressure, from highest to lowest.

Stack height combines four signals: emissions, GDP capacity, electricity access, and renewable gap.

Renewable gap is reversed: low renewable share creates a large green gap segment.

Click a color in the wheel or legend to isolate only that signal across all countries.

Selected countries glow and receive outer labels, so they can be compared against the whole world.

Hover a segment to see the raw value and its contribution.
