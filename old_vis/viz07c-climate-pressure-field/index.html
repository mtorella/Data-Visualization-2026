Climate Pressure Field

Countries behave like particles inside a climate-development force field. Four variables pull each country toward different pressure poles, revealing whether its profile is dominated by emissions, capacity, renewable gap, or access gap.

How to read

A map of competing pressures

Each dot is one country in the selected year.

Position is determined by four invisible pulls: emissions, GDP capacity, renewable gap, and electricity access gap.

Bigger dots have stronger combined transition pressure.

Color shows the strongest pressure in that country profile.

Near the center means the country is pulled by several pressures at once.
