Energy Bloom Timeline

This option sacrifices density for clarity and beauty. Instead of showing every country at once, it lets a few countries bloom through time so their energy identities become visually memorable.

Animated country portraits

Watch three energy identities bloom year by year

How to read

Petal length reflects GHG per person. Petal openness reflects electricity access. Green filaments reflect renewable share. The center disk grows with GDP per person.

Changing the year causes the same countries to re-bloom. This makes the visual identity of change more memorable than a standard line chart.
