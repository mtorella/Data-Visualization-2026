# Energy Bloom Timeline

This concept focuses on fewer countries at a time and turns each one into a bloom.

- petal length = GHG per capita
- petal openness = electricity access
- green filaments = renewable share
- core size = GDP per capita

Interactive feature:

- scrubbing the year slider causes the same selected countries to re-bloom through time

This option is the most poetic and the least crowded. It works well if you want the final piece to feel artistic and memorable.
