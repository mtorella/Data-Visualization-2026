Carbon Ladder

Countries do not all stand on the same rung of climate progress. This ladder asks whether a low-carbon profile comes with broad energy access and renewable strength, or whether it rests on weaker economic and infrastructure conditions.

Climbing the same ladder differently

Low emissions are not the whole story

How to read

Higher rungs indicate stronger transition conditions once emissions, access, renewables, and capacity are read together.

Leftward drift suggests that low emissions may rely more on constraint. Rightward drift suggests a cleaner transition with stronger access and capacity.

Bubble size shows total emissions. Hover any country to read its profile.
