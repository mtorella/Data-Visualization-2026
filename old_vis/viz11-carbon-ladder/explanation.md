# Carbon Ladder

This concept treats climate development as a ladder rather than a scatterplot.

- Higher rungs indicate stronger transition conditions after reading emissions, access, renewable share, and capacity together.
- Leftward drift means low emissions may be explained more by constraint.
- Rightward drift means low emissions are better supported by access and capacity.
- Bubble size shows total emissions.

Interactive feature:

- a lens slider changes how skeptical or optimistic the interpretation is
- a country selector highlights one national path through the ladder

This is creative, symbolic, and still easy to explain in a presentation.
