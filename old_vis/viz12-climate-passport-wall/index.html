Climate Passport Wall

Each country becomes a passport-style identity card. Instead of plotting dots, this view asks what kind of energy identity each country carries in a given year.

Identity cards, not data points

Who looks green by transition, and who only looks light because demand stays low?

Compare Two Passports

Click two cards to pin them side by side. The comparison is the interactive feature here: it turns countries into competing identity documents rather than anonymous marks.
