# Climate Passport Wall

This concept replaces points with passport-style cards.

- Each country is treated as an identity document.
- A narrative tag classifies it as transition, constraint, or carbon-heavy.
- The gold band scales with GDP.
- The seal displays renewable energy share.
- The passport body contains the core four variables.

Interactive feature:

- click any two cards to pin them in a side-by-side comparison panel

This is useful if you want something highly original and more editorial than chart-like.
