Carbon Tightrope

Countries balance between two interpretations: low carbon by necessity on one side, lower carbon through transition on the other. The rope becomes a moral spectrum.

Balancing act

Where each country lands on the rope changes with the lens

How to read

Left means low emissions are more plausibly read as limited access or capacity. Right means lower emissions are better supported by renewable and development conditions.

The amount a walker hangs below the rope reflects carbon pressure per person. Bigger walkers have larger total emissions.
