# Carbon Tightrope

This concept turns the main research question into a balancing act.

- Countries stand on a rope stretched between two interpretations.
- Left means low emissions are more plausibly linked to constraint.
- Right means low emissions are more plausibly linked to transition.
- The drop below the rope encodes GHG per capita.
- Walker size shows total emissions.

Interactive feature:

- a lens slider shifts the moral weighting of the rope in real time

This is one of the most metaphorical options while still remaining legible.
